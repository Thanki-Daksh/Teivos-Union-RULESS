# Teivos Union Rules

These rules are a starting point for keeping the server welcoming, fair, and fun. They apply to everyone playing on or taking part in the Teivos Union server community.

## Be respectful

- Treat other players with respect. Harassment, hate speech, threats, and targeted abuse are not welcome.
- Keep chat appropriate for a shared community space, and respect requests to stop disruptive behavior.
- Do not impersonate another player or staff member.

## Play fairly

- Do not use hacked clients, cheats, exploits, or unauthorized automation to gain an unfair advantage.
- Do not exploit bugs that harm the server or other players. Report them to the server team instead.
- Do not steal, grief, or alter another player’s builds or belongings without permission.

## Build and share responsibly

- Respect claimed areas, community builds, and agreed boundaries.
- Ask before borrowing, modifying, or relocating another player’s work.
- Keep shared areas accessible and follow any local signs or project guidelines.

## Help keep the server healthy

- Avoid actions that intentionally lag, crash, or disrupt the server.
- Follow reasonable directions from the server team about safety, moderation, and maintenance.
- Raise disagreements calmly with the server team rather than escalating them in public chat.

## Moderation

The server team may review reports and take action based on the situation, including warnings or removal from the server. If you believe a decision was mistaken, contact the team respectfully with relevant context.

These are community guidelines and may be updated as the group agrees on more specific server policies.
